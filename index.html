Personal Seller Research

This is a private seller-research helper used only by me.

It reads public listing search results (title, price, tags, favorites, views) across product categories I sell or am considering, so I can compare public market listings for my own shop. It does not edit listings, process sales, send emails, or store buyer data.

It is not a public product, not a commercial service, and not affiliated with Etsy, Inc.
